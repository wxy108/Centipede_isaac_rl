## 5. Body-weight correction (authoritative)

Single source of truth (also matches `pass11_overnight_BEST_ep12_bio_metrics.txt` and `sweep_phase3b_absolute_wave.py`):

- **m = 0.005 kg** (robot mass)
- **g = 9.81 m/s²**
- **W = m·g = 0.04905 N** (body weight)
- 5·W = 0.2453 N (impact-spike threshold inside the sweep code)

**The prior "15× body weight" claim is wrong by a factor of 10.** The correct ratio for the worst rough condition is:

- F_max_mean (over 5 trials) on λ=30 mm × abs_wave=1.5: **0.0730 N**
- F_max_mean / W = **1.49×** body weight (NOT 15×)
- Peak F_max across 5 trials / W on the same condition: **1.76×**

The 10× error was unit confusion (dividing F by mass-in-kg instead of weight-in-N). The corrections doc §2 explains the dimensional fault.

**Consistency check**: `impact_spike_count` (frames where F > 5·W = 0.245 N) is **0 across all 120 trials**. This is the right value given peak F_max ≈ 1.9·W; it would have been ~thousands of frames if the original "15× body weight" claim had been correct. The data was right; the text describing it was wrong.

Full corrected table per (terrain × abs_wave) is in `docs/PHASE_3B_AUDIT_CORRECTIONS_2026-05-11.md` §3.

## 8. Final scientific conclusion (corrected)

Phase 3B identifies a three-way trade-off at zero-action CPG with all other scales pinned at 1.0:

- **Speed**: abs_wave = **1.5** maximises forward velocity on every terrain (51 mm/s flat → 37 mm/s λ=30 mm).
- **Impact**: abs_wave = **4.0** minimises mean F_max on rough terrain. abs_wave = 1.5 carries an impact premium of **~1.3–1.5× body weight (mean)** / **~1.9× body weight (peak across 5 trials)** on λ ≤ 60 mm. **NOT 15× as previously stated.**
- **Stability**: abs_wave = **3.5** has the lowest CoV of v_fwd across rough terrains (CoV = 0.055).

**No single abs_wave wins all three.** Comparing the extremes (abs=1.5 vs abs=4.0 on rough):

| metric | abs=1.5 | abs=4.0 | premium of 1.5 over 4.0 |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| v_fwd_mean over rough (mm/s) | 43.34 | 30.75 | +12.59 mm/s (+41%) |
| F_max_mean/W over rough | 1.20× | 0.71× | 1.69× higher impact |
| CoV across rough | 0.138 | 0.059 | +0.079 (less stable) |

Interpretation: the speed gain at abs=1.5 is real (~13 mm/s mean over rough) and the impact premium (~1.7× the mean F_max) is also real but **modest in absolute terms** (peak still <2× body weight, never approaching the 5× impact-spike threshold). The stability cost is small but measurable (CoV +0.079).

The previous "15× body weight" framing **overstated the impact concern by an order of magnitude** and is retracted.
